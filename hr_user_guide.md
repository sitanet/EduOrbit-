# EduOrbit HRMS v1.1.0 — Master User Guide (`hr_user_guide.md`)

> **Module Version**: `v1.1.0-RELEASE`  
> **Target Audience**: All EduOrbit HRMS Users (Admins, Supervisors, Payroll Officers, Employees)

---

## 1. Introduction & Overview

EduOrbit HRMS v1.1.0 Enterprise Edition is a multi-tenant Human Resource Management System tailored for education enterprise networks. It unifies recruitment, onboarding identity verification (Dojah API), attendance tracking, statutory payroll (PAYE tax, Pension, NHF), performance appraisals, training, disciplinary compliance, rewards, and double-entry General Ledger integration.

---

## 2. Access Credentials & Navigation

### 2.1 Access Roles & Portals
- **HR Admin** (`hr.admin` / `Demo@2026`): Full Administrative Control (`/hr/admin/dashboard/`).
- **Payroll Specialist** (`payroll.admin` / `Demo@2026`): Payroll & GL Postings Console (`/hr/payroll/`).
- **Department Manager** (`dept.manager` / `Demo@2026`): Team Portal (`/hr/manager/team/`).
- **Staff Member** (`staff.member` / `Demo@2026`): Employee Self-Service (`/hr/ess/`).
- **Finance Officer** (`finance.officer` / `Demo@2026`): Finance GL Postings Log (`/hr/finance/postings/`).

---

## 3. Core Modules & User Instructions

### 3.1 Employee Self-Service (ESS)
- **Clock In / Out**: Click ⏰ Clock In terminal on top header. Grace period evaluates shift lateness automatically.
- **Leave Application**: Apply for casual, annual, or sick leave. Track 2-tier approval progress.
- **Payslip Download**: Access digital PDF payslips under `My Payslips`.

### 3.2 Staff Directory & Onboarding
- **Staff Roster**: Search, filter, and view staff profiles at `/hr/admin/directory/`.
- **Enterprise Onboarding Wizard**: Click `+ Add Staff Member (Enterprise Wizard)` at `/hr/admin/onboarding/wizard/` to complete the 8-Step onboarding flow.
