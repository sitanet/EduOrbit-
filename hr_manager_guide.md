# EduOrbit HRMS v1.1.0 — Department Manager & Supervisor Guide (`hr_manager_guide.md`)

> **Target Role**: Department Manager / Supervisor / HOD (`dept.manager`)  
> **Access Level**: Manager Team Portal  
> **Portal URL**: `/hr/manager/team/`

---

## 1. Manager Team Portal & Direct Reports

- **Navigation**: `/hr/manager/team/`
- **Dashboard Overview**: Displays direct reporting staff members, active leave requests, attendance summary metrics, and performance goal status.

---

## 2. Approving Leave Applications

1. Navigate to `/hr/manager/team/` → `Pending Approvals` Tab.
2. Review leave details (Employee Name, Dates, Reason, Impact on Department).
3. Actions:
   - Click **Approve (Level 1)**: Forwards request to HR Admin for final processing.
   - Click **Reject**: Input mandatory rejection reason and notify staff member.

---

## 3. Team Attendance & Shift Adjustments

- **Monitoring Shift Attendance**: View real-time clock-in times and lateness alerts for direct reports.
- **Attendance Adjustments**: Review and approve manual attendance adjustment requests submitted by staff members who missed clock-in registration.

---

## 4. Performance Appraisals & Goal Setting

- **Navigation**: `/hr/performance/`
- **Conducting Reviews**:
  1. Select direct report from team list.
  2. Input objective scores (1 to 5 scale) for established KPIs.
  3. Enter manager feedback and development comments.
  4. Submit appraisal for HR review.
